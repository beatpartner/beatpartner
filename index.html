常見問題

調音器為什麼要一直開著麥克風？

調音器要即時顯示演奏的音高，麥克風必須持續收音才做得到。 聲音只在手機上即時分析，分析完就丟掉，不會錄下來、不會存檔，也不會上傳。 切到其他 App 或鎖定螢幕時，調音器會停止收音。 詳見隱私權政策。

錄音和練習紀錄存在哪裡？會被上傳嗎？

全部存在使用者自己的手機裡，不會自動上傳。 Beat Partner 沒有帳號、沒有伺服器。使用者按匯出或分享時，檔案會交給 iPhone 的分享選單，由使用者決定傳到哪裡。

提醒：資料都在手機裡，所以刪除 App 會一起刪除全部內容。 重要的錄音，建議先用 App 內的備份功能匯出保存。

換手機時，資料可以搬過去嗎？

可以。在舊手機按「📤 匯出全量備份」產生備份檔， 再到新手機按「📥 匯入備份還原」載入。

匯入的音樂和播放器錄音放在音樂庫，要勾選「同時備份音樂庫」才會放進備份（預設不勾）。 完整版的購買不在備份檔裡，請見下一題。

換手機或重新安裝後，完整版要再買一次嗎？

不用。Beat Partner 完整版是一次付費，沒有訂閱，購買紀錄在 Apple。 用同一個 Apple 帳號，通常會自動解鎖；沒有的話，在設定的「完整版」那一列按「恢復購買」就好。

購買由 Apple 的 App Store 處理，Beat Partner 拿不到付款資料。

鎖螢幕或切到其他 App，節拍器會停嗎？

不會。Beat Partner 支援背景播放，鎖螢幕後節拍器會繼續響。 如果聲音中斷，請確認 iPhone 沒有開啟「低耗電模式」，也沒有其他 App 正在播放聲音。

可以和其他 App 的音樂一起播嗎？

目前不行。節拍器開始播放時，其他 App 的音樂會暫停，這樣鎖定畫面才能顯示節拍器的播放控制。

錄音沒有聲音，或聲音很小？

請依序確認：

  • iPhone「設定 → 隱私權與安全性 → 麥克風」裡，Beat Partner 是否已開啟
  • 手機沒有靜音，音量足夠
  • 使用藍牙耳機時，改用手機內建麥克風試試（部分藍牙耳機的麥克風品質較差）
調音器的音準和其他調音器不一樣？

請先確認 A4 基準頻率設定相同（多數樂器用 440 Hz，部分樂團用 442 Hz）。 也請在安靜的環境測試，環境噪音會影響偵測。

Frequently asked questions

Why does the tuner keep the microphone on?

To show the pitch being played in real time, the microphone has to keep listening. The sound is analyzed on the phone and discarded right away. It is not recorded, saved or uploaded. When the user switches to another app or locks the screen, the tuner stops listening. See the Privacy Policy.

Where are recordings and practice records stored? Are they uploaded?

Everything is stored on the user's own phone and is not uploaded automatically. Beat Partner has no accounts and no servers. When the user taps export or share, the file is handed to the iPhone share sheet, and the user decides where it goes.

Note: because all the data is on the phone, deleting the app deletes everything in it. For important recordings, export a backup from the app first.

Can the data be moved to a new phone?

Yes. On the old phone, tap "📤 匯出全量備份" (Export full backup) to create a backup file, then tap "📥 匯入備份還原" (Import backup) on the new phone to load it.

Imported music and player recordings are kept in the music library, and are included in the backup only if "同時備份音樂庫" (Also back up music library) is checked. It is unchecked by default. The full-version purchase is not part of the backup file; see the next question.

After switching phones or reinstalling, does the full version need to be bought again?

No. The Beat Partner full version is a one-time payment, not a subscription, and the purchase record is kept by Apple. With the same Apple Account, it usually unlocks automatically. If it does not, tap "恢復購買" (Restore Purchases) on the "完整版" (Full Version) row in Settings.

Purchases are handled by Apple's App Store; Beat Partner cannot obtain payment information.

Does the metronome stop when the screen is locked or another app is opened?

No. Beat Partner supports background playback, so the metronome keeps sounding when the screen is locked. If the sound stops, check that Low Power Mode is off and that no other app is playing audio.

Can it play along with music from other apps?

Not at the moment. When the metronome starts, music from other apps pauses, so that the lock screen can show the metronome's playback controls.

Recordings are silent or too quiet

Please check, in order:

  • Microphone access for Beat Partner is on in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone
  • The phone is not muted and the volume is up
  • With Bluetooth headphones, try the phone's built-in microphone instead (some Bluetooth microphones are lower quality)
The tuner reads differently from another tuner

First make sure the A4 reference is set the same (most instruments use 440 Hz; some orchestras use 442 Hz). Please also test in a quiet place, since background noise affects detection.

Privacy

Beat Partner does not collect any data from users: no accounts, no tracking, no ads. Purchases of the full version are handled by Apple's App Store; Beat Partner cannot obtain payment information. See the full Privacy Policy.
