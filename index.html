常見問題

調音器為什麼要一直開著麥克風？

調音器要即時顯示演奏的音高，麥克風必須持續收音才做得到。 聲音只在手機上即時分析，分析完就丟掉，不會錄下來、不會存檔，也不會上傳。 詳見隱私權政策。

錄音和練習紀錄存在哪裡？會被上傳嗎？

全部只存在使用者自己的手機裡，不會上傳到任何地方。 Beat Partner 沒有帳號、沒有伺服器，也不會連線傳送資料。

提醒：資料只在手機裡，所以刪除 App 會一起刪除全部內容。 重要的錄音，建議先用 App 內的備份功能匯出保存。

換手機時，資料可以搬過去嗎？

可以。在舊手機用 App 內的「備份」產生備份檔， 再到新手機用「還原」載入。

鎖螢幕或切到其他 App，節拍器會停嗎？

不會。Beat Partner 支援背景播放，鎖螢幕後節拍器會繼續響。 如果聲音中斷，請確認 iPhone 沒有開啟「低耗電模式」，也沒有其他 App 正在播放聲音。

可以和其他 App 的音樂一起播嗎？

可以。節拍器可以和其他 App 的聲音同時播放，例如一邊放伴奏一邊開節拍器。

錄音沒有聲音，或聲音很小？

請依序確認：

  • iPhone「設定 → 隱私權與安全性 → 麥克風」裡，Beat Partner 是否已開啟
  • 手機沒有靜音，音量足夠
  • 使用藍牙耳機時，改用手機內建麥克風試試（部分藍牙耳機的麥克風品質較差）
  • App 錄音頁的「輸入增益」是否調得太低
調音器的音準和其他調音器不一樣？

請先確認 A4 基準頻率設定相同（多數樂器用 440 Hz，部分樂團用 442 Hz）。 也請在安靜的環境測試，環境噪音會影響偵測。

Frequently asked questions

Why does the tuner keep the microphone on?

To show the pitch being played in real time, the microphone has to keep listening. The sound is analyzed on the phone and discarded right away. It is not recorded, saved or uploaded. See the Privacy Policy.

Where are recordings and practice records stored? Are they uploaded?

Everything stays on the user's own phone and is never uploaded. Beat Partner has no accounts and no servers, and does not send any data.

Note: because the data is only on the phone, deleting the app deletes everything in it. For important recordings, export a backup from the app first.

Can the data be moved to a new phone?

Yes. Create a backup file with "Backup" in the app on the old phone, then load it with "Restore" on the new phone.

Does the metronome stop when the screen is locked or another app is opened?

No. Beat Partner supports background playback, so the metronome keeps sounding when the screen is locked. If the sound stops, check that Low Power Mode is off and that no other app is playing audio.

Can it play along with music from other apps?

Yes. The metronome can play at the same time as other apps, for example together with a backing track.

Recordings are silent or too quiet

Please check, in order:

  • Microphone access for Beat Partner is on in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone
  • The phone is not muted and the volume is up
  • With Bluetooth headphones, try the phone's built-in microphone instead (some Bluetooth microphones are lower quality)
  • The "input gain" on the recording screen is not set too low
The tuner reads differently from another tuner

First make sure the A4 reference is set the same (most instruments use 440 Hz; some orchestras use 442 Hz). Please also test in a quiet place, since background noise affects detection.
