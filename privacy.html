隱私權政策

Beat Partner（節拍夥伴）· 生效日期：2026 年 7 月 27 日 · 最後更新：2026 年 9 月 28 日

Beat Partner 不會收集使用者的任何資料。使用者在 App 裡記下的內容，全部只存在使用者自己的手機裡。
Beat Partner 沒有帳號、不用登入，也沒有伺服器。App 不會自己連上網路傳送資料，裡面也沒有任何廣告或分析追蹤工具。

Beat Partner 收集哪些資料？

不收集任何資料。

不過，不收集不代表 App 裡沒有個人資料。使用者輸入或錄下的內容，例如名字、學生名單、練習紀錄、照片、錄音、文件，都存在使用者的手機上，由使用者自己保管。Beat Partner 看不到，也拿不到。

App 會使用哪些權限？

麥克風

  • 調音器：即時分析使用者演奏的音高。聲音分析完就丟掉，不會錄下來，也不會存成檔案。
  • 錄音：人聲報拍、多軌疊錄、播放器錄音、聯絡簿練習錄音。錄好的檔案只存在使用者的手機裡。
  • 錄音延遲校準：錄一小段測試聲音，量出手機錄音慢了多少。量完測試錄音就丟掉，只留下量到的數字。

相機

在聯絡簿拍照記錄練習，例如樂譜或姿勢。照片會先縮小再存進 App，只存在使用者的手機裡。

照片

在聯絡簿從相簿挑照片加進練習紀錄，App 只拿得到使用者挑的那幾張。把聯絡簿截圖存進相簿時，系統會先詢問一次。

這些權限都可以隨時在 iPhone 的「設定 → 隱私權與安全性」關閉。關閉後只有對應的功能不能用，其他照常。

背景播放

為了讓節拍器在切換到其他 App 或鎖定螢幕時繼續發聲，App 會使用 iOS 的背景播放。播放時，鎖定畫面會顯示「Beat Partner 節拍器」和目前的速度，只顯示在使用者的手機上。

資料存在哪裡？

全部存在使用者的 iPhone 裡，App 自己的儲存空間：

  • 節拍器、調音器的設定、編排和快照
  • 練習紀錄和練習時數
  • 聯絡簿的內容和附件（名字、學生名單、練習紀錄、留言、照片、文件、錄音）
  • 錄下的人聲、多軌錄音、播放器錄音
  • 匯入的音樂檔

另外還有錄音裝置的資訊。手機麥克風、USB 拾音器、耳機，每一種的錄音延遲和音量都不一樣，所以 App 會在手機裡記住裝置類型、裝置名稱，以及一串由裝置編號算出來、無法反推原本編號的代碼，換裝置時才能套用正確的設定。

App 不會自己把這些資料傳出去。

如果使用者開啟了 iCloud 備份，或用電腦備份 iPhone，iOS 可能會把 App 的資料一起放進那份備份。那份備份由 Apple（或使用者的電腦）依使用者的設定處理，Beat Partner 無法取得。

什麼時候資料會離開手機？

只有下面三種情況，而且每一種都要使用者自己操作。

1. 使用者按「回報問題／建議」

App 會打開郵件，預先填好一封寄給 beatpartner.app@gmail.com 的信。信裡有 App 版本、手機型號、系統版本、語言，以及 App 最近一次出錯的訊息（如果有的話），用來協助找出問題。

這封信使用者看得到、可以修改，也可以不寄。沒有按下寄出，就不會送出任何東西。信裡不包含錄音、照片或練習內容。如果手機沒有設定郵件，App 會改把同樣的文字複製起來，讓使用者貼到想用的地方。

2. 使用者自己匯出或分享

App 裡有些地方可以匯出資料，都是使用者按下後，交給 iPhone 的分享選單，由使用者決定傳到哪裡（AirDrop、郵件、雲端硬碟等）。Beat Partner 不會經手，也收不到。各種匯出包含：

  • 資料備份：幾乎所有 App 資料，包括聯絡簿、學生資料和附件、錄音；匯入的音樂可以選擇要不要放。備份檔也會記下是哪一台裝置做的備份，以及上面提到的錄音裝置資訊（還原到其他手機時，這些裝置資訊不會被套用）。
  • 聯絡簿：某一天的截圖，以及當天的照片、文件、錄音。
  • 學生傳給老師的練習回報：學生名字、要傳給的老師、練習紀錄、留言、附件，以及一組隨機產生的代碼，讓老師辨認是同一位學生。這組代碼和手機無關。
  • 老師回覆學生：老師和學生的名字、作業、備註、上課日期和時數，以及老師的附件。
  • 編排、資料夾、人聲設定（包含錄下的人聲）、多軌混音、錄音壓縮檔。

檔案傳出後，就由接收的一方（例如雲端服務，或收到檔案的人）依他們自己的規定處理。

3. Apple 的評分視窗

練習到一定次數後，App 可能會請 iPhone 顯示 Apple 的「為這個 App 評分」。要不要評分、寫什麼都由使用者決定，由 Apple 處理，App 不會因此送出任何資料。

他人的資料

聯絡簿可以在使用者自己的手機上記錄他人的資料，例如老師記錄學生。這些資料只存在使用者的手機裡，除非使用者自己匯出或分享，Beat Partner 沒有、也拿不到。

因此，保管這些資料是使用者的責任：

  • 請確認有記錄的正當理由，並依所在地的規定取得必要的同意；記錄的對象是未成年人時也一樣。
  • 匯出或分享含有他人資料的檔案時，請留意檔案會離開 App。
  • 學生傳練習回報給老師之前，請先確認內容。

Beat Partner 適合各年齡層使用。App 沒有帳號、社群、聊天或廣告，Beat Partner 不會收集任何未成年人的個人資料。

如何刪除資料？

資料都在使用者的手機裡，所以刪除 App，手機上的 App 資料就會一起刪除。已經匯出到 App 外的檔案，以及 iCloud 或電腦備份裡的舊資料，不會跟著刪除，需要使用者自己處理。

也可以在 App 裡逐一刪除錄音、練習紀錄、聯絡簿附件。Beat Partner 這邊沒有任何使用者資料需要刪除。

為了避免誤刪，多軌編排刪除後會先放進回收筒，要自己清空或永久刪除才會真正刪掉。單軌錄音和「覆蓋前自動備份」在回收筒最多保留 30 天，這類內容加起來超過 300 MB 時，會先清掉最舊的。

Privacy Policy

Beat Partner · Effective date: July 27, 2026 · Last updated: September 28, 2026

Beat Partner does not collect any data from users. Everything users record in the app stays only on their own phone.
Beat Partner has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers. The app does not connect to the internet to send data on its own, and it contains no advertising or analytics trackers.

What data does Beat Partner collect?

None.

That said, not collecting data does not mean there is no personal data in the app. What users type or record, such as names, student lists, practice records, photos, recordings and documents, is stored on the user's phone and kept by the user. Beat Partner cannot see it or obtain it.

Which permissions does the app use?

Microphone

  • Tuner: analyzes the pitch the user is playing in real time. The sound is discarded right after analysis. It is not recorded or saved as a file.
  • Recording: voice count-in samples, multitrack overdubs, player recordings and practice-notebook recordings. Recorded files are stored only on the user's phone.
  • Recording-latency calibration: records a short test sound to measure how late the phone records. The test recording is discarded after measuring; only the measured number is kept.

Camera

Used to take photos in the practice notebook, such as sheet music or posture. Photos are resized before being saved in the app and are stored only on the user's phone.

Photos

Used to add photos from the library to practice records. The app can only access the photos the user picks. When a notebook screenshot is saved to the library, the system asks once first.

Any of these permissions can be turned off at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security. Only the related feature stops working; everything else works as usual.

Background playback

So that the metronome keeps sounding when the user switches apps or locks the screen, the app uses iOS background audio. While it plays, the lock screen shows "Beat Partner 節拍器" and the current tempo, only on the user's phone.

Where is the data stored?

Entirely on the user's iPhone, in the app's own storage:

  • metronome and tuner settings, arrangements and snapshots
  • practice records and practice time
  • notebook entries and attachments (names, student lists, practice records, messages, photos, documents, recordings)
  • recorded voice samples, multitrack recordings and player recordings
  • imported music files

There is also recording-device information. The phone's microphone, a USB pickup and headphones each have different recording latency and volume, so the app remembers, on the phone, the device type, the device name, and a code derived from the device's identifier that cannot be turned back into the original identifier. This lets the right settings be applied when the device changes.

The app never sends any of this data out on its own.

If the user has iCloud Backup turned on, or backs up the iPhone to a computer, iOS may include the app's data in that backup. That backup is handled by Apple (or the user's computer) according to the user's settings; Beat Partner has no access to it.

When does data leave the phone?

Only in the three situations below, and each one requires the user to act.

1. The user taps "Report an issue / suggestion"

The app opens the mail app with a pre-filled message to beatpartner.app@gmail.com. It includes the app version, phone model, system version, language, and the app's most recent error message (if there is one), to help find the problem.

The user can read and edit this message, or choose not to send it. Nothing is sent until the user taps send. The message contains no recordings, photos or practice content. If no mail account is set up on the phone, the app copies the same text instead, so the user can paste it wherever they like.

2. The user exports or shares

Several places in the app can export data. Each one starts when the user taps it and is handed to the iPhone share sheet, where the user chooses the destination (AirDrop, Mail, cloud storage and so on). Beat Partner is not involved and never receives these files. What each export contains:

  • Data backup: almost all app data, including the notebook, student data and attachments, and recordings; imported music is optional. The backup also records which device created it, along with the recording-device information described above (that device information is not applied when restoring onto another phone).
  • Notebook: a day's screenshot, with that day's photos, documents and recordings.
  • A student's practice report to a teacher: the student's name, the teacher it is for, practice records, messages, attachments, and a randomly generated code that lets the teacher recognize the same student. The code has nothing to do with the phone.
  • A teacher's reply to a student: teacher and student names, assignments, notes, lesson date and duration, and the teacher's attachments.
  • Arrangements, folders, voice settings (including recorded voice samples), multitrack mixdowns and recording archives.

Once a file has been sent, it is handled by whoever receives it (for example a cloud service, or the person it was sent to) under their own rules.

3. Apple's rating prompt

After a certain amount of practice, the app may ask iPhone to show Apple's "rate this app" prompt. Whether to rate and what to write is up to the user and is handled by Apple; the app does not send any data because of it.

Other people's data

The notebook lets users record other people's data on their own phone, for example a teacher recording students. This data exists only on the user's phone. Unless the user exports or shares it, Beat Partner does not have it and cannot obtain it.

Keeping this data is therefore the user's responsibility:

  • Make sure there is a legitimate reason to record it, and obtain any consent required where you are, including when the people recorded are minors.
  • When exporting or sharing a file that contains other people's data, keep in mind that the file leaves the app.
  • Check a student's practice report before sending it to the teacher.

Beat Partner is suitable for all ages. The app has no accounts, social features, chat or advertising, and Beat Partner does not collect any personal information about minors.

How to delete data

All data is on the user's phone, so deleting the app deletes the app's data on the phone. Files already exported out of the app, and older data inside iCloud or computer backups, are not deleted with it and need to be handled by the user.

Recordings, practice records and notebook attachments can also be deleted one by one in the app. Beat Partner holds no user data that would need deleting.

To prevent accidental loss, deleted multitrack arrangements first go to the recycle bin and are only removed for good when the user empties the bin or deletes them permanently. Single-track recordings and "automatic backups before overwriting" stay in the bin for up to 30 days; when this kind of content goes over 300 MB, the oldest items are removed first.
