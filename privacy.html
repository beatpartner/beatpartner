beatpartner.app@gmail.com Privacy Policy Beat Partner · Effective date: July 27, 2026 · Last updated: September 11, 2026 In one sentence: Beat Partner does not collect any of your personal data. There are no accounts, no logins, no connections to our servers, and no analytics or advertising trackers of any kind. Everything you create in the app — settings, practice records, recordings, photos — stays on your own device. 1. What data do we collect? None. Beat Partner has no servers, requires no account, and does not transmit any information about you. All features run offline on your device. 2. Which permissions does the app use, and why? Permission Purpose Where the data goes Microphone (1) Tuner: real-time pitch detection (2) Recording: recording voice-count samples, practice takes, and multitrack overdubs (1) Nothing is recorded or stored. Audio is analyzed in real time and immediately overwritten; no file is ever created. (2) Recordings are created but stored only on your device, never uploaded anywhere. Camera Taking photos in the practice notebook (e.g. sheet music or posture) Stored only on your device, never uploaded Photo Library Adding existing photos to practice records Stored only on your device, never uploaded You may disable any permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security. Only the corresponding feature will stop working; the rest of the app continues normally. About the tuner's microphone use While the tuner is active, the microphone listens continuously so that pitch can be displayed in real time. The audio is analyzed on-device only and discarded immediately — never recorded, stored, or transmitted. 3. Where is your data stored? Entirely on your iPhone/iPad, in the app's private storage (localStorage and IndexedDB): metronome and tuner settings, practice records and streaks, notebook entries and attachments (photos, documents, audio), your recorded voice samples and multitrack takes, and any music files you import. This data is never collected by us and never leaves your device on its own, and we have no way to access it. The only two ways data can leave your device both require a deliberate action by you — see section 4 below. 4. Is data shared with third parties? No. Beat Partner contains no analytics tools (such as Google Analytics or Firebase), no advertising or tracking SDKs, no cross-app or cross-site tracking, and collects no identifiers for advertising purposes. The single exception: when you choose to report an issue If you tap "Report an issue / suggestion" in the app, your email client opens with a pre-filled message containing technical details such as app version, device model, OS version, and the mode you were using, to help us diagnose the problem. That email is entirely under your control — you can read it, edit it, or delete it without sending. Nothing leaves your device unless you press send. It contains none of your recordings, photos, or practice content. The other exception: when you export or share Several places in the app let you send data off the device, for example: the backup file produced by "Backup & restore" (which contains your recordings, photos and documents) notebook exports — screenshots, audio and documents exporting a single arrangement, a folder, or an audio file Every one of these is initiated by you and handed to the iOS share sheet, where you choose the destination (AirDrop, mail, cloud storage, another app…). We are not involved and never receive those files. Please note that once a file leaves the app it is no longer covered by this policy; it is handled by whichever service you sent it to, under that service's own policy. 5.
