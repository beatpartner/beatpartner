隱私權政策

Beat Partner（節拍夥伴）· 生效日期：2026 年 7 月 27 日 · 最後更新：2026 年 10 月 10 日

Beat Partner 不會收集使用者的任何資料。使用者在 App 裡記下的內容，全部存在使用者自己的手機裡，不會自動上傳。
Beat Partner 沒有帳號、不用登入，也沒有伺服器。App 不會自己把資料傳到網路上。跟購買有關的連線（顯示價格、購買完整版、恢復購買）由 iPhone 和 Apple 的 App Store 處理（見下面第 5 點）。App 裡沒有任何廣告或分析追蹤工具。

Beat Partner 收集哪些資料？

不收集任何資料。

不過，不收集不代表 App 裡沒有個人資料。使用者輸入或錄下的內容，例如名字、學生名單、練習紀錄、照片、錄音、文件，都存在使用者的手機上，由使用者自己保管。Beat Partner 看不到，也拿不到。

App 會使用哪些權限？

麥克風

  • 調音器：即時分析使用者演奏的音高。聲音分析完就丟掉，不會錄下來，也不會存成檔案。切到其他 App 或鎖定螢幕時，調音器會停止收音。
  • 錄音：人聲報拍、自錄音效、多軌疊錄、播放器錄音、聯絡簿練習錄音。錄好的檔案存在使用者的手機裡，不會自動上傳。
  • 在 Beat Lab 錄完音後，如果沒在播放，麥克風最多再開 30 秒；如果正在播放，會等播放停下來後再關。這段時間不會錄下任何聲音。切到其他 App 或鎖定螢幕時，會立刻關（正在錄音時除外）。
  • 錄音延遲校準：錄一小段測試聲音，量出手機錄音慢了多少。量完測試錄音就丟掉，只留下量到的數字。

相機

在聯絡簿拍照記錄練習，例如樂譜或姿勢。照片會先縮小再存進 App，存在使用者的手機裡，不會自動上傳。

照片

  • 在聯絡簿從相簿挑照片加進練習紀錄，App 只拿得到使用者挑的那幾張。
  • 在聯絡簿按「📸 儲存並截圖」時，截圖和當天的附件照片會交給 iPhone 的分享選單。使用者在選單裡選「儲存影像」，才會存進相簿；iOS 可能會先詢問是否允許加入照片。

這些權限都可以隨時在 iPhone 的「設定 → 隱私權與安全性」關閉。關閉後只有對應的功能不能用，其他照常。

背景播放

為了讓節拍器在切換到其他 App 或鎖定螢幕時繼續發聲，App 會使用 iOS 的背景播放。播放時，鎖定畫面會顯示「Beat Partner 節拍器」和目前的速度。這些顯示由 iOS 處理，App 不會把它傳到別的地方。

資料存在哪裡？

全部存在使用者的 iPhone 裡，App 自己的儲存空間：

  • 節拍器、調音器的設定、編排和快照
  • 練習紀錄和練習時數
  • 聯絡簿的內容和附件（名字、學生名單、練習紀錄、留言、照片、文件、錄音）
  • 錄下或匯入的人聲、自訂音效、多軌錄音、播放器錄音
  • 匯入的音樂檔
  • 回收筒裡還沒清掉的錄音
  • App 自己的運作紀錄，例如音訊出錯時的技術紀錄、功能使用次數（不含使用者輸入或錄下的內容）
  • 有沒有買完整版，以及智能定音免費試用已經用了多久

另外還有錄音裝置的資訊。手機麥克風、USB 拾音器、耳機，每一種的錄音延遲和音量都不一樣，所以 App 會在手機裡記住裝置類型、裝置名稱和分辨裝置用的代碼，換裝置時才能套用正確的設定。代碼有兩種：

  • 一種由裝置編號算出來，無法反推原本的編號；
  • 另一種是 iOS 提供給 App、用來記住調音器上次選哪一支麥克風的代碼。

App 不會自己把這些資料傳出去。

如果使用者開啟了 iCloud 備份，或用電腦備份 iPhone，iOS 可能會把 App 的資料一起放進那份備份。那份備份由 Apple（或使用者的電腦）依使用者的設定處理，Beat Partner 無法取得。

什麼時候資料會離開手機？

只有下面五種情況。前四種都要使用者自己操作；第 5 種跟購買有關，由 Apple 處理。

1. 使用者按「回報問題／建議」

App 會打開郵件，預先填好一封寄給 beatpartner.app@gmail.com 的信。信裡有 App 版本、手機型號、系統版本、語言，以及 App 最近一次出錯的訊息（如果有的話），用來協助找出問題。

這封信使用者看得到、可以修改，也可以不寄。沒有按下寄出，就不會送出任何東西。信裡不會附上錄音、照片或練習內容。錯誤訊息是 App 的技術訊息，使用者寄出前看得到，也可以刪掉。如果手機沒有設定郵件，App 會改把同樣的文字複製起來，讓使用者貼到想用的地方。

2. 使用者自己匯出或分享

App 裡有些地方可以匯出資料。都是使用者按下後，交給 iPhone 的分享選單，由使用者決定傳到哪裡（AirDrop、郵件、雲端硬碟等）。Beat Partner 不會經手，也收不到。各種匯出包含：

  • 資料備份：
    • 內容：幾乎所有 App 資料，包括聯絡簿、學生資料和附件、錄音。
    • 音樂庫：匯入的音樂和播放器錄音放在音樂庫，要勾選「同時備份音樂庫」才會放進備份（預設不勾）。
    • 裝置資訊：備份檔會記下一段標示裝置種類和系統版本的文字（例如 iPhone 或 iPad），不含能辨認是哪一支手機的編號，也會記下上面提到的錄音裝置資訊；還原到其他手機時，這些裝置資訊不會被套用。
    • 備份檔沒有加密；裡面如果有他人的資料，請存放在可信任的地方。
  • 聯絡簿：某一天的截圖，以及當天的照片、文件、錄音；也可以只分享其中一個附件，或把文件交給其他 App 打開。
  • 學生傳給老師的練習回報：
    • 學生名字、要傳給的老師；
    • 全部日期的練習紀錄，包括時數、心情，以及老師之前寫的作業和備註；
    • 留言、附件；
    • 一組隨機產生的代碼，讓老師辨認是同一位學生。這組代碼和手機無關。
  • 老師回覆學生：老師和學生的名字、作業、備註、上課日期、時數和心情，以及老師的附件。
  • 編排、資料夾：會連同裡面的多軌錄音一起匯出。
  • 人聲和自訂音效設定：包含錄下的聲音。
  • 其他：多軌混音；播放器裡的音檔（一個檔，或多個檔的壓縮檔）。

檔案傳出後，就由接收的一方（例如雲端服務，或收到檔案的人）依他們自己的規定處理。

3. Apple 的評分

練習到一定次數後，或使用者按「為這個 App 評分」時，App 可能會請 iPhone 顯示 Apple 的評分視窗，或打開 App Store 的評論頁。要不要評分、寫什麼都由使用者決定，由 Apple 處理，App 不會因此送出任何資料。

4. 使用者按「隱私權政策」

App 會用手機的瀏覽器打開這一頁，不會附帶任何資料。和一般上網一樣，放這一頁的網站（GitHub Pages）會收到連線資訊，例如 IP 位址。

5. 購買完整版、恢復購買

  • 購買完整版由 Apple 的 App Store 處理。付款方式、信用卡和 Apple 帳號的資料都在 Apple，Beat Partner 拿不到，也看不到。
  • App 只會從 iPhone 得知「這個 Apple 帳號有沒有買完整版」，用來打開進階功能。這個結果存在使用者的手機裡，不會上傳。
  • 顯示價格、購買、按「恢復購買」時，iPhone 會和 Apple 的 App Store 連線；iPhone 平常也可能自己和 App Store 同步購買紀錄。這些連線由 Apple 處理，不經過 Beat Partner；Beat Partner 沒有自己的伺服器。
  • 換手機或重新安裝時，用同一個 Apple 帳號，完整版通常會自動回來；沒有的話，在「設定 › 完整版」按「恢復購買」就好。
  • 智能定音的免費試用（總共 10 分鐘）用了多少，只記在使用者的手機裡。
  • 購買、退款和 Apple 帳號的資料，依 Apple 的隱私權政策處理。

他人的資料

聯絡簿可以在使用者自己的手機上記錄他人的資料，例如老師記錄學生。這些資料存在使用者的手機裡，不會自動上傳，Beat Partner 沒有、也拿不到。

因此，保管這些資料是使用者的責任：

  • 請確認有記錄的正當理由，並依所在地的規定取得必要的同意；記錄的對象是未成年人時也一樣。
  • 匯出或分享含有他人資料的檔案時，請留意檔案會離開 App。
  • 學生傳練習回報給老師之前，請先確認內容。

Beat Partner 適合各年齡層使用。App 沒有帳號、社群、聊天或廣告，Beat Partner 不會收集任何未成年人的個人資料。未成年人購買完整版時，如果家人共享群組開了「購買前詢問」，要由群組的管理者在 Apple 那邊核准。

如何刪除資料？

資料都在使用者的手機裡，所以刪除 App，手機上的 App 資料就會一起刪除。已經匯出到 App 外的檔案，以及 iCloud 或電腦備份裡的舊資料，不會跟著刪除，需要使用者自己處理。

刪除 App 不會取消購買。購買紀錄在 Apple，重新安裝後用同一個 Apple 帳號，完整版通常會自動回來；沒有的話，按「恢復購買」就好。

也可以在 App 裡逐一刪除錄音和聯絡簿附件，或清空某一天練習紀錄的內容。老師移除學生時，這位學生的紀錄、照片、錄音和文件會一起刪除。Beat Partner 這邊沒有任何使用者資料需要刪除。

為了避免誤刪，多軌編排刪除後會先放進回收筒，要自己清空或永久刪除才會真正刪掉。單軌錄音和「覆蓋前自動備份」在回收筒最多保留 30 天；這類內容加起來超過 300 MB 時，會先清掉最舊的。

Privacy Policy

Beat Partner · Effective date: July 27, 2026 · Last updated: October 10, 2026

Beat Partner does not collect any data from users. Everything users record in the app is stored on their own phone and is not uploaded automatically.
Beat Partner has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers. The app does not send data to the internet on its own. Connections related to purchases (showing the price, buying the full version, restoring purchases) are handled by the iPhone and Apple's App Store (see item 5 below). The app contains no advertising or analytics trackers.

What data does Beat Partner collect?

None.

That said, not collecting data does not mean there is no personal data in the app. What users type or record, such as names, student lists, practice records, photos, recordings and documents, is stored on the user's phone and kept by the user. Beat Partner cannot see it or obtain it.

Which permissions does the app use?

Microphone

  • Tuner: analyzes the pitch the user is playing in real time. The sound is discarded right after analysis. It is not recorded or saved as a file. When the user switches to another app or locks the screen, the tuner stops listening.
  • Recording: voice samples for counting beats, custom sound effects, multitrack overdubs, player recordings and practice-notebook recordings. Recorded files are stored on the user's phone and are not uploaded automatically.
  • After recording in Beat Lab, if nothing is playing, the microphone stays on for up to 30 seconds; if something is playing, it turns off after playback stops. Nothing is recorded during this time. When the user switches to another app or locks the screen, it turns off right away (except while recording).
  • Recording-latency calibration: records a short test sound to measure how late the phone records. The test recording is discarded after measuring; only the measured number is kept.

Camera

Used to take photos in the practice notebook, such as sheet music or posture. Photos are resized before being saved in the app, and are stored on the user's phone and not uploaded automatically.

Photos

  • Used to add photos from the library to practice records in the practice notebook. The app can only access the photos the user picks.
  • When the user taps "📸 儲存並截圖" (Save and take screenshot) in the practice notebook, the screenshot and that day's attached photos are handed to the iPhone share sheet. They are saved to the photo library only if the user chooses "Save Image" there; iOS may first ask whether to allow adding photos.

Any of these permissions can be turned off at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security. Only the related feature stops working; everything else works as usual.

Background playback

So that the metronome keeps sounding when the user switches apps or locks the screen, the app uses iOS background audio. While it plays, the lock screen shows "Beat Partner 節拍器" and the current tempo. This display is handled by iOS; the app does not send it anywhere else.

Where is the data stored?

Entirely on the user's iPhone, in the app's own storage:

  • metronome and tuner settings, arrangements and snapshots
  • practice records and practice time
  • notebook entries and attachments (names, student lists, practice records, messages, photos, documents, recordings)
  • recorded or imported voice samples, custom sound effects, multitrack recordings and player recordings
  • imported music files
  • recordings in the recycle bin that have not been cleared yet
  • the app's own operating records, such as technical logs when an audio error occurs and feature usage counts (these contain nothing the user typed or recorded)
  • whether the full version has been purchased, and how much of the free Smart Tuning (智能定音) trial has been used

There is also recording-device information. The phone's microphone, a USB pickup and headphones each have different recording latency and volume, so the app remembers, on the phone, the device type, the device name and codes that tell devices apart. This lets the right settings be applied when the device changes. There are two kinds of code:

  • one is derived from the device's identifier and cannot be turned back into the original identifier;
  • the other is a code that iOS gives the app, used to remember which microphone the tuner used last.

The app does not send any of this data out on its own.

If the user has iCloud Backup turned on, or backs up the iPhone to a computer, iOS may include the app's data in that backup. That backup is handled by Apple (or the user's computer) according to the user's settings; Beat Partner has no access to it.

When does data leave the phone?

Only in the five situations below. The first four require the user to act; the fifth relates to purchases and is handled by Apple.

1. The user taps "回報問題／建議" (Report an issue / suggestion)

The app opens the mail app with a pre-filled message to beatpartner.app@gmail.com. It includes the app version, phone model, system version, language, and the app's most recent error message (if there is one), to help find the problem.

The user can read and edit this message, or choose not to send it. Nothing is sent until the user taps send. No recordings, photos or practice content are attached. The error message is a technical message from the app; the user can see it before sending and can delete it. If no mail account is set up on the phone, the app copies the same text instead, so the user can paste it wherever they like.

2. The user exports or shares

Several places in the app can export data. Each one starts when the user taps it and is handed to the iPhone share sheet, where the user chooses the destination (AirDrop, Mail, cloud storage and so on). Beat Partner is not involved and never receives these files. What each export contains:

  • Data backup:
    • Contents: almost all app data, including the notebook, student data and attachments, and recordings.
    • Music library: imported music and player recordings are kept in the music library, and are included in the backup only if "同時備份音樂庫" (Also back up music library) is checked. It is unchecked by default.
    • Device information: the backup records a line of text showing the device type and system version (for example, iPhone or iPad), with no number that could identify which phone it is, along with the recording-device information described above. That device information is not applied when restoring onto another phone.
    • Backup files are not encrypted. If one contains other people's data, please keep it somewhere trustworthy.
  • Notebook: a day's screenshot, with that day's photos, documents and recordings. A single attachment can also be shared on its own, or a document can be opened in another app.
  • A student's practice report to a teacher:
    • the student's name and the teacher it is for;
    • practice records for all dates, including practice time, mood, and the assignments and notes the teacher wrote earlier;
    • messages and attachments;
    • a randomly generated code that lets the teacher recognize the same student. The code has nothing to do with the phone.
  • A teacher's reply to a student: teacher and student names, assignments, notes, lesson date, duration and mood, and the teacher's attachments.
  • Arrangements and folders: exported together with the multitrack recordings in them.
  • Voice and custom sound-effect settings: including the recorded sounds.
  • Other: multitrack mixdowns; audio files in the player (a single file, or a compressed archive of several files).

Once a file has been sent, it is handled by whoever receives it (for example a cloud service, or the person it was sent to) under their own rules.

3. Apple ratings

After the user has practiced a certain number of times, or when the user taps "為這個 App 評分" (Rate this app), the app may ask iPhone to show Apple's rating prompt, or open the App Store review page. Whether to rate and what to write is up to the user and is handled by Apple; the app does not send any data because of it.

4. The user taps "隱私權政策" (Privacy Policy)

The app opens this page in the phone's browser and does not attach any data. As with any website, the site hosting this page (GitHub Pages) receives connection information, such as the IP address.

5. Buying the full version and restoring purchases

  • Purchases of the full version are handled by Apple's App Store. Payment methods, credit cards and Apple Account details stay with Apple; Beat Partner cannot obtain or see them.
  • The app only learns from the iPhone whether this Apple Account has bought the full version, and uses that to unlock the advanced features. This result is stored on the user's phone and is not uploaded.
  • When the price is shown, when buying, and when the user taps "恢復購買" (Restore Purchases), the iPhone connects to Apple's App Store; the iPhone may also sync purchase records with the App Store on its own. These connections are handled by Apple and do not go through Beat Partner; Beat Partner has no servers of its own.
  • When switching phones or reinstalling with the same Apple Account, the full version usually comes back automatically. If it does not, tap "恢復購買" (Restore Purchases) in 設定 › 完整版 (Settings › Full Version).
  • How much of the free Smart Tuning trial (10 minutes in total) has been used is recorded only on the user's phone.
  • Purchase, refund and Apple Account data are handled under Apple's privacy policy.

Other people's data

The notebook lets users record other people's data on their own phone, for example a teacher recording students. This data is stored on the user's phone and is not uploaded automatically; Beat Partner does not have it and cannot obtain it.

Keeping this data is therefore the user's responsibility:

  • Make sure there is a legitimate reason to record it, and obtain any consent required by local rules, including when the people recorded are minors.
  • When exporting or sharing a file that contains other people's data, keep in mind that the file leaves the app.
  • Check a student's practice report before sending it to the teacher.

Beat Partner is suitable for all ages. The app has no accounts, social features, chat or advertising, and Beat Partner does not collect any personal information about minors. When a minor buys the full version and the Family Sharing group has "Ask to Buy" turned on, the group's organizer needs to approve it with Apple.

How to delete data

All data is on the user's phone, so deleting the app deletes the app's data on the phone. Files already exported out of the app, and older data inside iCloud or computer backups, are not deleted with it and need to be handled by the user.

Deleting the app does not cancel the purchase. The purchase record is kept by Apple; after reinstalling with the same Apple Account, the full version usually comes back automatically. If it does not, just tap "恢復購買" (Restore Purchases).

Recordings and notebook attachments can also be deleted one by one in the app, and the contents of a day's practice record can be cleared. When a teacher removes a student, that student's records, photos, recordings and documents are deleted together. Beat Partner holds no user data that would need deleting.

To prevent accidental loss, deleted multitrack arrangements first go to the recycle bin and are only removed for good when the user empties the bin or deletes them permanently. Single-track recordings and "automatic backups before overwriting" stay in the bin for up to 30 days; when this kind of content goes over 300 MB, the oldest items are removed first.
